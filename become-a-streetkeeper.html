Three Ways to
Become a Streetkeeper

Option A

Church Sponsored Access

Join through your local church using the access code they provide.

  • Enjoy full access to the app and the map. Claim, cover, and release streets.
  • Share the vision with friends, family, pastors, and churches. Links to the Prayer Cover demo and also the Pastor's Kit, which explains church participation, can be found on the Share the Vision page.

Option B - $7.99 / month

Individual Streetkeeper

  • Participate independently, even if your church is not participating.
  • Use Away From Home to temporarily claim and cover one additional street while traveling or away from home. Scattered Seeds keeps a history of the places you've prayed.
  • Receive an additional 10-day reusable Guest Pass so others can truly experience Prayer Cover.
  • Receive 15% off Streetkeeper Gear.
  • Share the vision with friends, family, pastors, and churches.
  • Sign up below to begin as an Individual Streetkeeper.

Option C — $14.99 / month

Streetkeeper Partners

  • Includes two Streetkeepers under one shared subscription.
  • Each Streetkeeper receives their own access and can claim, cover, and release streets in any U.S. city of their choosing.
  • Each Streetkeeper Partner receives their own Away From Home entitlement to temporarily claim and cover an additional street while traveling or away from home. Scattered Seeds keeps a history of the places they've prayed.
  • Includes two reusable 10-day Guest Passes so others can truly experience Prayer Cover.
  • Receive 15% off Streetkeeper Gear.
  • Sign up below to begin as Streetkeeper Partners.
